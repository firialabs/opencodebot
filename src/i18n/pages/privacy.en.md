# Privacy policy

*Effective December 3, 2026. Last updated December 3, 2026.*

{app} is Firia Labs' free, open-source editor and simulator for the CodeBot, CodeX and CodeAIR.
It is made to be used by students, including children under 13, so it is built to collect nothing
about them.

This policy covers {app} only: the app at this address and its open-source code. CodeSpace
(make.firialabs.com) and the Firia Labs store (firialabs.com) have their own privacy policy, at
[firialabs.com/pages/privacy-policy](https://firialabs.com/pages/privacy-policy), as will
{app} Classroom when it opens.

## What we collect

Nothing. {app} has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for a name, an email address or
anything else about the person using it. It sets no cookies, and it has no ads, no analytics, no
error reporting service and no tracking of any kind, ours or anyone else's.

## Where your work goes

Your programs and settings stay with you:

- **In this browser.** {app} saves your programs, the blocks you keep in My blocks, the mission
  packs you open and your place in them, and your settings (language, layout, sound levels) in this
  browser's own storage on this computer. They never leave it. Clearing the browser's data for the
  site removes them.
- **In a folder you choose.** In Chrome and Edge you can open a folder on your computer and keep your
  programs there. {app} reads and writes only the folder you pick, and only while you let it.
- **On your board.** When you press Run with a board plugged in, your program is copied to the board
  over the USB cable. It goes from the browser straight to the board.

The simulator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you do in it is sent anywhere.

## What any web page does

Opening {app} loads it from the site that hosts it, as any web page does. The site is hosted by
Google's Firebase Hosting. Like any web server, its servers receive each request, including the
computer's network address and the browser it is using, and Google may keep these in its logs to
run and protect the service. Firia Labs does not turn on access to those logs, and uses no other
Google or Firebase product that collects information (no Google Analytics). The site tells the
other sites it links to nothing about where a visitor came from.

After the first visit, {app} works offline: the browser keeps a copy, and later visits need no
connection.

## Our commitments to students and schools

Because {app} collects nothing, these follow from how it is built; we state them because schools
ask:

- No advertising, and no information used to target it.
- No profile of any student, for advertising or anything else.
- Nothing to sell, share or disclose: we hold no information about students.
- A school can use {app} without sharing student records with Firia Labs.
- If we ever change this, it will be in a new version of this policy first (see Changes).

## Links to other sites

{app} links to a few other sites: Firia Labs' device documentation (docs.firialabs.com), GitHub,
where anyone can report a problem or look at the code, and, in some lessons, pages that tell more
about what the lesson covers. Those links open in a new tab, and only when you click them. Those
sites have their own privacy policies. Reporting a problem on GitHub needs a GitHub account, and
what you write there is public.

## Children

{app} collects no personal information from anyone, so it collects none from children under 13,
and there is nothing for a parent or teacher to review or delete with us. A student's programs are
in their browser, their folders and their board, where they, a parent or a teacher can see, keep or
delete them at any time.

## Your rights

Wherever you live, including in the European Union and the UK, you have rights over personal
information a company holds about you. Firia Labs holds none from your use of {app}. If you have a
question about it, write to [dpo@firialabs.com](mailto:dpo@firialabs.com).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its date. We will not
start collecting information about the people using {app} without saying so here first.
